Purpose & Vision

The purpose of SMART Greenhouse is to enable sustainable crop production in drought-prone regions through the use of AI, automation, and water-conservation technologies. When completed, it will provide a self-regulating horticultural facility that minimizes water consumption, improves plant health, and serves as an educational resource for schools and community organizations.

SMART Objectives

  • Construct a small-scale greenhouse structure capable of supporting year-round crops.
  • Integrate 5 core smart technologies: Mobile App, Irrigation, AI Monitoring, Solar, and Website.
  • Reduce water consumption by at least 30% compared to traditional manual methods.

Community & Education

Serving communities in drought-prone regions, schools, and community organizations. The greenhouse serves as a hands-on STEM learning resource, demonstrating how artificial intelligence and sustainable farming practices work together to support growers without replacing human oversight.

Irrigation Logic & Drip Mechanics

The Fully Automated Irrigation System integrates soil moisture sensors placed strategically across the root zones. When moisture levels drop below the preset threshold (40%), solenoid valves open automatically, delivering targeted drip irrigation directly to plant roots. This prevents over-watering, minimizes evaporation loss in arid environments, and guarantees compliance with our 30% water savings success criteria.

How AI Supports Growers

The AI Plant Health monitoring system demonstrates how artificial intelligence can support sustainable agriculture by helping growers make better decisions, conserve resources, and improve crop health. Rather than replacing human farmers and gardeners, AI acts as an assistant tool that monitors environmental conditions, identifies potential problems early, and provides actionable recommendations.

1

Early Problem Identification

Detects subtle chlorosis or leaf wilting days before visible to the naked eye.

2

Actionable Recommendations

Suggests precise fertilizer or humidity adjustments tailored to specific crop strains.

3

Predicted Harvest

Estimates yield forecasts (~120 kg/cycle), optimal harvest windows (12-14 days), and crop readiness using predictive environmental models.

Resource Planning

Resource Plan & Budget Description

Labor requirements, contractor allocation limits (25% max), and budget allocation.

Labor & Contractor Plan

The project utilizes both internal core team members and specialized external contractors. In strict adherence to project constraints, **only 25% of project work may be contracted out**. Contractors are strictly retained for specialized structural design and heavy greenhouse assembly, while our core team manages software development, AI monitoring integration, and stakeholder education.

Budget Description

The total capital budget is allocated across structural materials (40%), smart sensor hardware & AI integration (25%), solar renewable energy systems (15%), contractor fees (20%, adhering to the 25% work limit), and community contingency reserves. Funding is supported by county grants and environmental organization sponsorships.
